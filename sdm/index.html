    Choose a species and settings, then fit a model. The fit runs on the hexsdm server; regional species take under a minute, continent-wide ranges several minutes. Identical requests return the stored result immediately.

    Suitability is relative habitat suitability from presence-only data. Cross-validation compares presences with background cells, not surveyed absences, so the modelled range is a potential distribution, not a confirmed one.
